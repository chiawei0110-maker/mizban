Mizban is a friendly helper program that lets you connect to popular AI services like ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek from one single place. ## ⚡ Quick Troubleshooting

**Problem: Can't connect to an AI service**
Check your API key. Copy-paste it again from the provider's website. Make sure you have credits on your account.

**Problem: Mizban is slow**
Close browser tabs you don't need. Restart Mizban. Check your internet connection speed.

**Problem: Forgot my admin password**
Look for the reset folder in the Mizban directory. Follow the simple instructions inside.

**Problem: I want to change my language**
Go to Settings → Language → Pick your preference. Available in English, Persian, and more.
